OSINT Citation Generator v2.1

Pick a source type and fill the eight slots. The footnote assembles in house order with its punctuation, and slots that do not apply drop out along with theirs.

Author, "Title," Location [Description] (Publisher: Date) URL/DOI: Locator.

Source type

Sets the title style, the usual description, and which rules get checked.

1 Author

2 Title

Keep the source's own capitalization. Reproduce an error and follow it with [sic].

Non-English or translated source

Put the original-language title in the Title field. The translation follows it in brackets.

Credit a published translator in the contributors box with (trans.).

3 Location

The publication, platform, site, series, or archive that holds the source. Ten words or fewer. Always italic.

4 Description the bracket, never dropped

Browse the library

Pick the most specific description that fits. If nothing fits, write a plain description of ten words or fewer and send a note so it can be added.

Volume and issue, report number, episode, edition, resolution and coordinates. Separate items with semicolons.

Qualifiers

A qualifier describes this copy of the source, not its kind: [Field Manual; leaked; scan]. It follows the details in the bracket. Language and access have their own controls.

Access

Tick whatever stands between the reader and the source. Nothing ticked means free to read, which adds nothing. It goes in the bracket after the details and before the rating.

Source rating optional; click again to clear

Letters rate the source. Numbers rate the information.

5 Publisher

Who issued it. Leave blank for journals, newspapers, magazines, and wires. A publisher that repeats the location is dropped for you.

6 Date

Any common format becomes DD Month YYYY. Slashes are read as month/day/year. A year alone stays a year. Blank becomes n.d.

7 URL or DOI

Full link, never shortened. A bare DOI is rewritten as https://doi.org/….

8 Locator

Numbers get p. or pp. and an en dash. Where there are no pages, name the section (Article 4(2)) or give the timestamp (See 30:15–32:40 for …). Never count paragraphs.

After the citation

A caveat, how a date or location was established, or what could not be confirmed.

Surname for later citations. Filled in for you; correct it for names like Xi Jinping.
